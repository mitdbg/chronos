|---|---|---|
| Correctness | Bounds virtual requests by `max_num_batched_tokens`; covers all three buffers | Computes exact observed request/page requirements |

| Acceptance criteria | Best fit | Meets coverage intent, but GPU overflow behavior remains unvalidated |
