### Policy revisions

- v1 — interim support rule:
  - Content not persisted by default.
  - Opt-in debug payloads: 7 days.
  - Metadata-only operational logs and no-payload traces: 14 days.

- v3 — corrected canonical rule at `/knowledge/policies/hosted-api-retention.md`:
  - Retains the v1 default-retention claims.
  - Hosted erasure/termination: primary purge within 30 days; backups and replicated snapshots within 90 days; attestation available on completion, subject to holds and contracts.
  - Configured allowed-region sets constrain customer-content processing and content-bearing logs and prevent out-of-region fallback.
  - Unpinned accounts have no residency guarantee.
  - Backup locality remains contract/SLA-dependent.
  - Replica deletion uses tombstones, DeletionEvent state, restore guards, and per-replica acknowledgements; typical internal timings are not presented as general SLAs.
