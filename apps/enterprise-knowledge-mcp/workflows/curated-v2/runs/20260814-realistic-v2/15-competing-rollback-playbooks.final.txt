Published exactly one policy: the evidence-gated candidate.

## Candidates

1. Fast recovery — rejected

- Field operator immediately routes 100% to the last-known-good version.
- No prerequisite evidence or approval gate during active impact.
- Uses the latest available snapshot if needed.
- Validates latency, errors, and smoke tests for 15 minutes.
- Collects evidence and approvals after restoration.

Primary weakness: it assumes universal operator authority, snapshot compatibility, and sufficient network state. Those assumptions conflict with approval, evidence-preservation, and restore-safety guidance.

2. Evidence-gated — accepted

- Immediately pauses rollout and permits IC-authorized traffic isolation for imminent harm.
- Requires a pre-mutation evidence package covering telemetry, provenance, snapshots, schema compatibility, and—when applicable—network plans, routes, peering, DNS, and reachability.
- Requires IC/SRE authorization plus applicable Release Owner, Security/Evidence Custodian, and customer-operator approvals.
- Prohibits bypassing non-reversible downgrade gates.
- Restores state only after snapshot identity, checksum, schema compatibility, and invariants are verified.
- Requires 60 continuous minutes at baseline before closure.
- Defines explicit stop-and-escalate conditions.

This is the selected policy as written, not a hybrid with the fast candidate.
